  <p class="guide-intro">Minimalism isn't about owning nothing — it's about making room for what matters. This guide covers digital decluttering, reducing screen time, and simplifying your physical space.</p>

## Digital Declutter

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>File Organization</h4>
    <p>Use a consistent folder structure: <code>~/Projects/Year/Project</code> or <code>~/Archive/Type/Item</code>. Delete duplicates, rename files descriptively, and archive old projects you rarely access.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Desktop Cleanup</h4>
    <p>Zero desktop = zero distraction. Move everything into organized folders. Use your desktop only for temporary files that get cleared daily. Your wallpaper should actually be visible.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Email Zero</h4>
    <p>Unsubscribe from newsletters you never read. Use filters to auto-label incoming mail. Aim for inbox zero by archiving or responding immediately. Schedule email checks rather than constant refreshing.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>App Audit</h4>
    <p>Go through your phone and computer. Delete apps you haven't used in 30 days. Uninstall games you don't play. Remove clutter from your dock/taskbar. Fewer choices = less decision fatigue.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Browser Cleanup</h4>
    <p>Close tabs you're not actively using. Use bookmarks sparingly (or use Raindrop.io). Remove extensions you don't need. Limit your bookmark bar to daily-use tools only.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Cloud Storage Audit</h4>
    <p>Go through Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud. Delete old screenshots, duplicate files, and documents you no longer need. Organize what's left into clear folders. You'll find GBs of trash.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Reducing Screen Time

<div class="resource-grid">
  <div class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Grayscale Mode</h4>
      <p>Turn your phone to grayscale in accessibility settings. Colorful apps like Instagram become less engaging. You'll instinctively reach for your phone less often.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Remove Social Apps</h4>
      <p>Delete TikTok, Instagram, Twitter from your phone. Use them only on desktop if needed. This alone can reclaim hours of your day.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>App Blockers</h4>
      <p>Use Freedom, Cold Turkey, or 1Focus to block distracting apps/sites during work hours. Set a schedule and stick to it.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Phone Downgrade</h4>
      <p>Consider a dumb phone (Light Phone, Punkt) or use a minimalist launcher (Olauncher, Niagara). Break the addiction at the source.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>No-Phone Zones</h4>
      <p>No phones in the bedroom (use an alarm clock), at the dinner table, or for the first 30 minutes after waking. Set physical boundaries.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Digital Sabbath</h4>
      <p>Pick one day per week (or a few hours) with no screens. Read a book, go for a walk, talk to people in person. Reset your relationship with technology.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## Physical Minimalism

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>One In, One Out</h4>
    <p>For every new item you bring in, remove one old item. Keeps your possessions at a sustainable level and makes you think twice before buying.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>KonMari Method</h4>
    <p>Keep only what sparks joy. Go by category (clothes, books, papers), not by location. Thank each item before letting it go.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>The 30-Day Rule</h4>
    <p>For non-essential purchases over $50, wait 30 days before buying. Most impulse purchases feel unnecessary after the waiting period.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Quality Over Quantity</h4>
    <p>Buy fewer, better things. A $200 pair of boots that lasts 10 years beats $50 boots that last 1 year. Cheaper in the long run, less waste.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Minimalist Mindset

<div class="about-tip reveal">
  <span class="tip-icon">🧠</span>
  <span><strong>The goal is not to own nothing.</strong> The goal is to own only what serves you. Every item you own should either be useful or beautiful. If it's neither, it's clutter. Be intentional about what you let into your space and your life.</span>
</div>
