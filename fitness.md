### The Three Pillars of Fitness

| Pillar | What It Is | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **Strength** | Ability to exert force | Bone density, metabolism, daily function |
| **Cardio** | Heart & lung efficiency | Endurance, heart health, mental clarity |
| **Mobility** | Range of motion + flexibility | Injury prevention, pain-free movement |

### The Minimal Viable Routine

If you have no routine today, start here:

| Day | Activity | Duration |
|-----|----------|----------|
| Monday | Bodyweight strength (squats, push-ups, planks) | 20 min |
| Tuesday | Walking or light cardio | 30 min |
| Wednesday | Bodyweight strength | 20 min |
| Thursday | Walking or light cardio | 30 min |
| Friday | Bodyweight strength + stretching | 25 min |

> **The #1 Rule:** Show up. A "bad" workout is infinitely better than no workout.


## 🏋️ Strength Training {#strength}

> **Source:** [ExRx.net](https://exrx.net) — one of the most comprehensive, evidence-based exercise libraries on the internet.

### Bodyweight Exercises (No Equipment)

| Movement | Muscles Worked | Beginner | Intermediate |
|----------|---------------|----------|--------------|
| **Squat** | Quads, glutes, core | 3x10 | 3x20 |
| **Push-up** | Chest, shoulders, triceps | Knee push-ups 3x8 | Standard 3x15 |
| **Lunge** | Legs, glutes, balance | 3x8 per leg | 3x15 per leg |
| **Plank** | Core, shoulders | 3x20 sec | 3x60 sec |
| **Glute Bridge** | Glutes, hamstrings, low back | 3x12 | 3x20 |
| **Reverse Snow Angel** | Upper back, posture | 3x10 | 3x20 |
| **Side Lying Leg Lift** | Hip abductors, glute medius | 3x10 per side | 3x15 per side |

### How to Progress (The 2-for-2 Rule)

When you can complete **2 more reps** than your target on the **last 2 sets** across consecutive workouts, increase difficulty:

| Progression Method | Example |
|--------------------|---------|
| Add reps | 3x10 → 3x12 |
| Add sets | 3x10 → 4x10 |
| Slow the tempo | 3x10 (2 sec down) → (4 sec down) |
| Reduce rest | 60 sec rest → 45 sec rest |
| Add weight/resistance | Bodyweight → Band → Dumbbell |


## 🏃 Cardio & Conditioning {#cardio}

### The Health Guidelines

| Organization | Recommendation |
|--------------|----------------|
| **WHO** | 150-300 min moderate OR 75-150 min vigorous cardio weekly |
| **AHA** | 150 min moderate activity weekly |
| **CDC** | 150 min moderate + 2 strength days weekly |

### Cardio Options (From Easiest to Hardest)

| Activity | Intensity | Equipment | Best For |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Walking | Light | Shoes only | Beginners, recovery days |
| Incline walking | Moderate | Treadmill or hill | Low-impact fat burning |
| Cycling | Moderate to high | Bike (stationary or outdoor) | Joint-friendly cardio |
| Swimming | Moderate to high | Pool | Full body, zero impact |
| Running | High | Shoes only | Time-efficient cardio |
| Rowing | High | Rowing machine | Full body + cardio |
| Jump rope | Very high | Rope | Coordination + cardio |

### The "Talk Test" for Intensity

| Intensity | What It Feels Like | Example |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Light** | Can sing while doing it | Easy walk |
| **Moderate** | Can talk but not sing | Brisk walk, light jog |
| **Vigorous** | Can't say more than a few words | Sprint, hard row |

### LISS vs. HIIT

| Type | What It Is | Frequency | Best For |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **LISS** (Low-Intensity Steady State) | Sustained moderate effort, 30-60 min | 3-5x/week | Beginners, recovery, fat loss foundation |
| **HIIT** (High-Intensity Interval Training) | Short bursts of max effort + rest, 15-20 min | 1-2x/week | Time-efficient, metabolic boost |

> ⚠️ **Warning:** HIIT is stressful on the body. Limit to 1-2 sessions per week and never do back-to-back days.

## 🧘 Flexibility & Mobility {#mobility}

### Why Mobility Matters

Flexibility without control = hypermobility (injury risk). Mobility = strength **through** a range of motion.

### Essential Daily Mobility Routine (5-10 min)

| Exercise | Purpose | Hold/Reps |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Cat-Cow** | Spine mobility | 10 slow reps |
| **World's Greatest Stretch** | Hip + spine + shoulder | 5 reps per side |
| **Thoracic Rotation** | Upper back mobility | 8 reps per side |
| **Deep Squat (hold)** | Ankle + hip mobility | Hold 30-60 sec |
| **Pigeon Pose** | External hip rotation | Hold 30 sec per side |
| **Neck CARs** (Controlled Articular Rotations) | Neck mobility | 5 slow reps each direction |

## 🍎 Nutrition Basics {#nutrition}

> Strength is built in the gym. Body composition is built in the kitchen.

### The Simple Plate Method (No Counting Required)

Fill your plate for any meal:

* **½ plate**: Vegetables (Broccoli, spinach, peppers, carrots, salad)
* **¼ plate**: Protein (Chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, beans, Greek yogurt)
* **¼ plate**: Carbohydrates (Rice, potatoes, quinoa, oats, whole grain bread)
* **+**: Healthy fat (Olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds)

## 🛌 Recovery & Sleep {#recovery}

### Signs You Need a Rest Day

- 😴 Waking up tired despite adequate sleep
- 🦵 Persistent muscle soreness lasting >72 hours
- 😠 Irritability or mood swings
- 📉 Performance plateau or decline
