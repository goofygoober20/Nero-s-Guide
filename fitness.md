### How to Progress (The 2-for-2 Rule)

When you can complete **2 more reps** than your target on the **last 2 sets** across consecutive workouts, increase difficulty:

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Add reps</h4>
    <p>3×10 → 3×12</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Add sets</h4>
    <p>3×10 → 4×10</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Slow the tempo</h4>
    <p>3×10 (2 sec down) → (4 sec down)</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Reduce rest</h4>
    <p>60 sec rest → 45 sec rest</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Add weight</h4>
    <p>Bodyweight → Band → Dumbbell</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Cardio & Conditioning {#cardio}

### Health Guidelines

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>WHO</h4>
    <p>150-300 min moderate OR 75-150 min vigorous cardio weekly</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>AHA</h4>
    <p>150 min moderate activity weekly</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>CDC</h4>
    <p>150 min moderate + 2 strength days weekly</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Cardio Options (From Easiest to Hardest)

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Walking</h4>
    <p>Light intensity. Shoes only. Beginners, recovery days</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Incline Walking</h4>
    <p>Moderate intensity. Treadmill or hill. Low-impact fat burning</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Cycling</h4>
    <p>Moderate to high. Bike (stationary or outdoor). Joint-friendly</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Swimming</h4>
    <p>Moderate to high. Pool. Full body, zero impact</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Running</h4>
    <p>High intensity. Shoes only. Time-efficient cardio</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Rowing</h4>
    <p>High intensity. Rowing machine. Full body + cardio</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Jump Rope</h4>
    <p>Very high. Rope. Coordination + cardio</p>
  </div>
</div>

### LISS vs. HIIT

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card" style="border-left: 3px solid #27ae60;">
    <h4>LISS</h4>
    <p><strong>Low-Intensity Steady State:</strong> Sustained moderate effort, 30-60 min. 3-5×/week. Best for beginners, recovery, fat loss foundation</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card" style="border-left: 3px solid #e74c3c;">
    <h4>HIIT</h4>
    <p><strong>High-Intensity Interval Training:</strong> Short bursts of max effort + rest, 15-20 min. 1-2×/week. Time-efficient, metabolic boost</p>
  </div>
</div>

::: warning
HIIT is stressful on the body. Limit to 1-2 sessions per week and never do back-to-back days.
:::

    <p>Hip + spine + shoulder. 5 reps per side</p>

    <p>Upper back mobility. 8 reps per side</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Deep Squat Hold</h4>
    <p>Ankle + hip mobility. Hold 30-60 sec</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Pigeon Pose</h4>
    <p>External hip rotation. Hold 30 sec per side</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">

    <p>Neck mobility. 5 slow reps each direction</p>
  </div>
</div>
