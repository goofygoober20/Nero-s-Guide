## The Pomodoro Technique

<div class="shared-card reveal">
  <p>The Pomodoro Technique is simple: work for <strong>25 minutes</strong>, take a <strong>5-minute break</strong>. After four pomodoros, take a longer break (15-30 minutes). This cycle helps maintain focus and prevents burnout.</p>
</div>

<div class="steps-grid">
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Pick a Task</strong>
      <p>Choose one thing to work on. No multitasking — commit to this single task for one pomodoro.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Set a Timer</strong>
      <p>25 minutes. Close distractions. Use a physical timer or the <a href="/tools/study-tracker">Study Session Tracker</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Work With Focus</strong>
      <p>No interruptions. If something comes up, write it down and deal with it after the timer ends.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Take a Break</strong>
      <p>Step away from your desk. Stretch, walk, get water. Let your brain reset before the next round.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## Spaced Repetition (Anki)

<div class="shared-card reveal">
  <p><strong>Anki</strong> is a flashcard app that uses spaced repetition — showing you cards right before you'd forget them. It's the most efficient way to learn and retain information long-term.</p>
</div>

## Note-Taking Systems

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Zettelkasten</h4>
    <p>Create atomic notes that link to each other. Each note captures one idea. Build a web of knowledge over time. Tools: Obsidian, Logseq.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>PARA Method</h4>
    <p>Organize by actionability: Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives. Everything goes into one of these four categories. By Tiago Forte.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Cornell Notes</h4>
    <p>Divide your page into cues, notes, and summary. Great for lectures and meetings. Review cues to test yourself later.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="resource-grid">
  <a href="https://obsidian.md/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Obsidian</h4>
      <p>Local-first, markdown-based note-taking. Graph view shows connections between notes. Plugin ecosystem is enormous. Free.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
  <a href="https://www.notion.so/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Notion</h4>
      <p>All-in-one workspace. Notes, databases, kanban boards, wikis. Great for personal and team use. Free for personal use.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
  <a href="https://logseq.com/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Logseq</h4>
    <p>Open source, local-first. Outliner-based with block-level references. Great for Zettelkasten and journaling.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
  <a href="https://standardnotes.com/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Standard Notes</h4>
      <p>End-to-end encrypted notes app. Simple, focused, and private. Free tier or paid for extensions (markdown, code editor).</p>
    </div>
  </a>
</div>

## Task Management

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>GTD (Getting Things Done)</h4>
    <p>Capture everything in an inbox, clarify what it means, organize by context, review weekly, and do. The gold standard methodology.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Kanban Boards</h4>
    <p>Visualize your workflow: To Do, Doing, Done. Limit work in progress. Tools: Trello, GitHub Projects, Notion, Todoist.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Eisenhower Matrix</h4>
    <p>Quadrant-based: Urgent/Important, Not Urgent/Important, Urgent/Not Important, Not Urgent/Not Important. Prioritize accordingly.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Time Blocking</h4>
    <p>Schedule every part of your day in blocks. Put deep work first (morning), meetings in the afternoon. Google Calendar or any calendar app.</p>
  </div>
</div>
