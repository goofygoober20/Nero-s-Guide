## 🚀 Quick Start {#quick-start}

**TL;DR:** Start with [Drawabox](https://drawabox.com) — it's a free, structured course that teaches the fundamentals. Practice 15-30 minutes daily. Don't worry about making "good" art; focus on consistent practice.

### The 3 Most Important Fundamentals for Beginners

| Fundamental | What It Is | Best Free Resource |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Line quality** | Drawing smooth, confident lines | Drawabox Lesson 1 |
| **Form & perspective** | Making flat shapes look 3D | Drawabox Lesson 2-3 |
| **Light & shadow** | Understanding value and shading | Ctrl+Paint's Value series |

### The Daily Practice Habit

| Time Commitment | What to Do |
|----------------|------------|
| **15 minutes** | Warm-up lines, ellipses, boxes (from Drawabox) |
| **30 minutes** | Above + gesture drawing (Line of Action) |
| **60 minutes** | Above + focused study (hands, faces, anatomy) |

> **The secret:** Draw from life and references, not just imagination. Your brain's visual library needs real-world input.
