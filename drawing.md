## Quick Start {#quick-start}

<div class="about-tip">
  <GuideIcon name="info" class="tip-icon" />
  <span><strong>TL;DR:</strong> Start with <a href="https://drawabox.com" target="_blank">Drawabox</a> — it's a free, structured course that teaches the fundamentals. Practice 15-30 minutes daily. Don't worry about making "good" art; focus on consistent practice.</span>
</div>

### The 3 Most Important Fundamentals for Beginners

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Line Quality</h4>
    <p>Drawing smooth, confident lines. <strong>Best free resource:</strong> Drawabox Lesson 1</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Form &amp; Perspective</h4>
    <p>Making flat shapes look 3D. <strong>Best free resource:</strong> Drawabox Lesson 2-3</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>Light &amp; Shadow</h4>
    <p>Understanding value and shading. <strong>Best free resource:</strong> Ctrl+Paint's Value series</p>
  </div>
</div>

### The Daily Practice Habit

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>15 minutes</h4>
    <p>Warm-up lines, ellipses, boxes (from Drawabox)</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>30 minutes</h4>
    <p>Above + gesture drawing (Line of Action)</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4>60 minutes</h4>
    <p>Above + focused study (hands, faces, anatomy)</p>
  </div>
</div>

<blockquote style="font-style: normal; border-left: 4px solid var(--vp-c-brand-1); padding-left: 1rem; margin: 1rem 0;">
<strong>The secret:</strong> Draw from life and references, not just imagination. Your brain's visual library needs real-world input.
</blockquote>
