## Browser Hardening

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Firefox Hardening</h4>
    <p>Enable <strong>Enhanced Tracking Protection</strong> (Strict), set <code>privacy.firstparty.isolate</code> to true, install <strong>uBlock Origin</strong>, and use <strong>Firefox Multi-Account Containers</strong>. Consider <strong>LibreWolf</strong> for a pre-hardened fork.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Chrome/ium Hardening</h4>
    <p>Use <strong>uBlock Origin</strong>, enable <strong>Do Not Track</strong>, disable third-party cookies, use <strong>HTTPS Everywhere</strong>. Consider <strong>Brave</strong> for a privacy-focused Chromium-based browser with built-in blockers.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="resource-grid">
  <a href="https://ublockorigin.com/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>uBlock Origin</h4>
      <p>The gold standard ad/tracker blocker. Blocks ads, trackers, malware domains, and more. Available for Firefox and Chrome.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
  <a href="https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/containers" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Firefox Containers</h4>
      <p>Isolate your browsing sessions. Keep Facebook, Google, shopping, and work in separate containers so they can't track each other.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
  <a href="https://brave.com/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>Brave Browser</h4>
      <p>Chromium-based with built-in ad/tracker blocking, fingerprinting protection, and Tor mode. Privacy-focused out of the box.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
  <a href="https://librewolf.net/" target="_blank" class="resource-card reveal">
    <div class="resource-body">
      <h4>LibreWolf</h4>
      <p>Pre-hardened Firefox fork. Comes with uBlock Origin, strict privacy settings, and telemetry disabled. Highly recommended.</p>
    </div>
  </a>
</div>

## Email Privacy

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <a href="https://proton.me/mail" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Proton Mail</h4>
    <p>End-to-end encrypted email based in Switzerland. Free tier includes 1GB storage and 150 messages/day. Apps for iOS and Android.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://tuta.com/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Tuta (formerly Tutanota)</h4>
    <p>End-to-end encrypted email with encrypted calendar. Open source, based in Germany. Free tier includes 1GB storage.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://simplelogin.io/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>SimpleLogin / Addy.io</h4>
    <p>Email aliases that forward to your real inbox. Create unique addresses for every service so you can block them individually if they get spam or breached.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT210425" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Apple Hide My Email</h4>
    <p>If you're on Apple devices, iCloud+ includes random email address generation integrated into Safari and Sign in with Apple.</p>
  </a>
</div>

## VPNs

<div class="warning-card reveal">
  <strong>Important</strong>
  <p>A VPN is not a magic privacy solution. It hides your IP from websites but shifts trust to your VPN provider. Always choose a no-logs provider.</p>
</div>

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <a href="https://mullvad.net/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Mullvad VPN</h4>
    <p>Top recommendation. No email required — generate an account number. Accepts cash. No logs. Open source clients. €5/month.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://protonvpn.com/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Proton VPN</h4>
    <p>From the makers of Proton Mail. Free tier available (no logs, but slower). Paid plans include port forwarding and higher speeds.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://www.ivpn.net/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>IVPN</h4>
    <p>No logs, open source, accepts Monero and cash. Slightly pricier but highly transparent. Multi-hop available.</p>
  </a>
</div>
