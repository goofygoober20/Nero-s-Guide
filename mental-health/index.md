## Introduction

I read a lot, and learning more about thought patterns and questioning a lot about what goes on in my head led me down the path to find how other people deal with mental health. I've had friends and family deal with mental health struggles, to the point where it's debilitating. Some can't hold down a job because of the mental health issues, and some can't even concentrate as they are completely stricken by anxiety and can't leave the house. It's with the hope that this information helps them lead more balanced, fruitful lives.

A lot of what is written below comes from Dr. Julie Smith, and her bestselling books *Why Has Nobody Told Me This Before?* and *Open When..*. I've read the books from front to back and highlighted the things that stand out to me in my personal life. Dr. Smith has helped me astronomically in my personal struggles, with concepts that are easy to understand. I owe a lot to them for my personal development, and I'm hoping this helps you too.

If you enjoy what is written, I would recommending buying her books, and subscribing to her <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@DrJulie" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">YouTube channel</a>. I believe that education and knowledge should be free, and easily distributed throughout the world, but I also understand some have to make a living from the knowledge that they possess.

## Let's start with something really important: you matter.

Your thoughts, feelings, and well-being are just as important as anything else in your life. But honestly, taking care of your mental health can be challenging. Life moves fast, and there's always more to do. Stress can catch up with you unexpectedly. Before you know it, you might feel exhausted, questioning when you last felt like yourself.

This guide is meant for those times when you need some support. It's not designed to bombard you with advice or simply suggest that you "just think positive."

Instead, it's a reminder that you're not alone in what you're going through. Everyone has their own struggles. Everyone deals with challenges that others may not notice. That doesn't mean you're weak; it means you're human.

Inside, you'll discover helpful tips, simple habits, and gentle reminders that your mental health needs attention, just like the care you give to everything and everyone around you. This isn't about changing yourself because you're not broken. It's about creating room in your life for rest, reflection, and personal growth.

Feel free to pick what feels right for you from this guide, and ignore what doesn't. There's no perfect way to care for your mind - just what suits you best. Remember, even the tiniest step you take is still progress.

You're stronger than you might believe. You're doing better than you realize. And you are definitely worth the effort it takes to feel good, both inside and out.
