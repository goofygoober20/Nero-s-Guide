# Unenrollment Guide

<div class="guide-hero">
  <p class="guide-meta"><GuideIcon name="pencil" :size="14" /> Written by <strong>Nero</strong></p>
  <p class="guide-intro">This guide applies to school-issued Chromebooks or devices managed by institutional policies. It is intended to help students understand the context and risks before using any Titanium Network unenrollment resources.</p>
  <p class="guide-intro">This tool uses <a href="https://docs.titaniumnetwork.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Titanium Network</a> to batch unenrollment methods and create simplified instructions for the approach you want to use.</p>
</div>

<div class="warning-card">
  <strong>⚠️ Warning</strong>
  <p>Unenrolling a school-managed Chromebook may violate your school's acceptable use policy and could result in disciplinary action. This guide is for educational purposes. You are responsible for understanding the consequences before proceeding. Unenrolling may also void any support or warranty provided by your school or district.</p>
</div>
