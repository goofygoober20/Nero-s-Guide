## 🚀 Quick Start: The 20-Piece Capsule Wardrobe {#capsule}

> **Source:** Capsule Wardrobe guide [citation:1]

**What is a capsule wardrobe?** A small collection of versatile, high-quality pieces that all work together. The concept dates back to the 1970s, and it's making a comeback because it saves money, space, and decision fatigue [citation:1][citation:6].

### The 3 Golden Rules

| Rule | Why It Matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Choose versatile colors** | Neutrals (black, white, gray, beige, navy) as a base + 1-2 seasonal accent colors [citation:1] |
| **Match your actual life** | Dress for *your* routine—work, errands, travel—not a fantasy version of yourself [citation:1] |
| **Prioritize quality & fit** | Each piece should work for multiple occasions. A crisp white shirt works for the office *and* weekend jeans [citation:1][citation:2] |

### The 20 Essential Pieces

| Category | Item | Why You Need It |
|----------|------|-----------------|
| **Tops** | Plain white/black/gray T-shirts (2-3) | The ultimate layering piece [citation:6] |
| | Classic white button-down | Works everywhere—office to beach [citation:1][citation:2] |
| | Neutral knit sweater | Cozy but polished [citation:7] |
| | Tank top or camisole | Layering or hot days [citation:6] |
| **Bottoms** | Well-fitting jeans (dark wash) | Day or night, casual or dressed up [citation:1][citation:7] |
| | Tailored trousers or chinos | Office or smart-casual [citation:2][citation:7] |
| | Versatile skirt (midi or A-line) | Feminine but practical |
| | Comfortable shorts | Warm weather [citation:1] |
| **Dresses** | Little black dress | The ultimate "I don't know what to wear" solution [citation:7] |
| | Casual shirt or knit dress | One-and-done outfit [citation:1] |
| **Outerwear** | Classic blazer | Polished in 30 seconds [citation:1][citation:6][citation:7] |
| | Trench coat or long coat | Timeless, works over everything [citation:1][citation:6] |
| | Denim or leather jacket | Edgy, casual layer [citation:6] |
| **Shoes** | Clean white sneakers | Everyday, everywhere [citation:2][citation:6] |
| | Loafers or ballet flats | Smart-casual comfort [citation:2] |
| | Ankle boots | Year-round staple [citation:2][citation:6] |
| | Minimal heels | For nights out [citation:6] |
| **Accessories** | Simple leather belt | Pulls any outfit together [citation:7] |
| | Watch or minimal jewelry | Polished finish [citation:2] |
| | Quality bag or tote | Carries your life [citation:2][citation:7] |

### How to Start

1. **Audit your current closet** — Pull out everything. Which of these 20 pieces do you already own? [citation:1]
2. **Identify gaps** — What's missing? What's worn out?
3. **Shop strategically** — Buy the best quality you can afford for the pieces you'll wear most.
4. **Test drive** — For the next 2 weeks, only wear pieces from your capsule. Notice what you reach for and what you don't [citation:1].

## 👤 Dressing for Your Body Type {#body-type}

> **Source:** Body type styling guides [citation:3][citation:8]

Fit is the most important factor in looking good. Expensive clothes look cheap if they don't fit. Inexpensive clothes look expensive if they do [citation:8].

### Identify Your Shape

| Body Type | Characteristics | Goal | What to Wear |
|-----------|----------------|------|--------------|
| **Hourglass** | Bust and hips balanced, defined waist | Accentuate curves | Wrap dresses, belted styles, fitted tops |
| **Pear** | Hips wider than shoulders | Balance proportions | A-line skirts, statement tops/necklines, dark bottoms |
| **Apple** | Broader shoulders/bust, less defined waist | Create length, define waist | V-necks, empire waists, straight-leg pants |
| **Rectangle** | Shoulders and hips aligned, minimal waist definition | Create curves | Peplum tops, belted jackets, high-waisted bottoms |

### Fit Fundamentals

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Shoulders first** | For tops and jackets, shoulder seams should hit at your natural shoulder bone. Everything else can be tailored; shoulders can't [citation:2]. |
| **Hemlines matter** | Pants should just graze the top of your shoes. Skirts should hit at the most flattering point on your leg (usually just above or below the knee). |
| **Avoid "baggy + baggy"** | Balance proportions: loose on top? Fitted on bottom. Fitted on top? Loose on bottom [citation:3]. |

## 🎨 Color & Personal Style {#color-style}

> **Source:** Color and style guides [citation:3][citation:8]

### Finding Your Colors

| Undertone | Flattering Colors | Colors to Be Careful With |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Warm** (yellow/golden undertone) | Earth tones, olive, cream, camel, rust | Pastels, icy blues |
| **Cool** (pink/blue undertone) | Jewel tones, true white, navy, lavender | Orange, mustard yellow |
| **Neutral** | Almost everything | Very bright neons |

> **Quick test:** Look at the veins on your wrist. Greenish = warm undertone. Bluish = cool undertone. Both = neutral [citation:3].

### Finding Your Personal Style

| Style Vibe | Key Pieces | Avoid |
|------------|------------|-------|
| **Minimalist** | Clean lines, neutral colors, quality fabrics | Logos, excessive hardware, trendy pieces |
| **Classic** | Tailored pieces, pearls, trench coats, loafers | Extreme silhouettes, fast fashion |
| **Bohemian** | Flowing fabrics, earth tones, layered jewelry | Structured, stiff garments |
| **Edgy** | Leather, hardware, black-on-black, unique silhouettes | Pastels, florals, overly sweet details |

> **Pro tip:** Create a mood board (Pinterest or even just a folder of screenshots) of outfits you love. After 20-30 images, patterns will emerge about what you're actually drawn to [citation:8].

### The "Confidence Rule"

The best outfit you can wear is the one that makes you feel like yourself. No matter how trendy or expensive your clothing may be, if you don't feel good in it, that will show. Confidence is the finishing touch that enhances any outfit [citation:8].

## 🌱 Ethical & Sustainable Fashion {#ethical}

> **Sources:** PETA sustainable fashion guide [citation:5] and Harper's BAZAAR sustainable brands [citation:10]

### Why It Matters

The fashion industry is one of the largest polluters in the world. Fast fashion encourages overconsumption, poor working conditions, and massive waste [citation:5][citation:10]. Sustainable fashion focuses on:

- **Ethical production** — Fair wages, safe working conditions
- **Eco-friendly materials** — Organic cotton, recycled materials, plant-based alternatives
- **Slow fashion** — Quality over quantity, pieces designed to last

### Sustainable & Vegan Clothing Brands

| Brand | Focus | Price Point |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Stella McCartney** | Luxury, no leather/fur, sharp tailoring | High-end [citation:5][citation:10] |
| **Another Tomorrow** | B Corp certified, supply chain transparency | High [citation:10] |
| **Deiji Studios** | Linen loungewear, biodegradable fabrics | Mid-High [citation:10] |
| **House of Dagmar** | Scandinavian minimalist, sustainable | Mid-High [citation:10] |
| **B Sides** | Upcycled denim, made from repurposed Levi's | Mid [citation:10] |
| **Nour Hammour** | Leather jackets (leather is agricultural byproduct) | High [citation:10] |
| **Collina Strada** | Deadstock fabrics, made in NYC | Mid-High [citation:10] |
| **Ninety Percent** | Donates 90% of profits to causes & workers | Mid [citation:10] |
| **Worn Out Vegan** | Ethical vegan apparel, donates 10% to animal charities | Mid [citation:5] |
| **Harvest & Mill** | Organic cotton, dye-free or natural dyes, made in USA | Mid [citation:5] |
| **Ccilu** | Shoes made from recycled coffee grounds & plastic bottles | Low-Mid [citation:5] |
| **WAMA Underwear** | Hemp underwear (sustainable, antibacterial) | Mid [citation:5] |

### How to Shop More Sustainably (On Any Budget)

| Action | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **Buy secondhand** | Thrift stores, Depop, Poshmark, Vinted — gives clothes a second life [citation:10] |
| **Repair what you have** | Learn basic mending (sewing a button, fixing a hem) or take it to a tailor |
| **Wash less, wash colder** | Most clothes don't need washing after every wear. Cold water saves energy and fabric [citation:9] |
| **Choose natural fibers** | Cotton, linen, wool, hemp are biodegradable. Polyester will sit in a landfill for 200+ years |
| **Ignore "greenwashing"** | Look for certifications (B Corp, Oeko-Tex, 1% for the Planet) rather than vague claims [citation:10] |

## 🧼 Fabric Care Guide {#fabric-care}

> **Sources:** Comprehensive fabric care guides [citation:4][citation:9]

**The Golden Rule:** Always check the care label first. Those little symbols are there for a reason [citation:9].

### Care by Fabric Type

| Fabric | Washing | Drying | Special Care |
|--------|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Cotton** | Machine wash warm/cold. Wash darks with darks, lights with lights. | Tumble dry low or air dry. Remove promptly to avoid wrinkles. | Can shrink in high heat. Iron while slightly damp for crisp finish [citation:4][citation:9] |
| **Linen** | Gentle cycle or hand wash. Cool water. Skip fabric softener. | Air dry flat or hang. Linen dries quickly. | Wrinkles are part of its charm! Steam or iron damp on medium-high [citation:4][citation:9] |
| **Wool** | Wash rarely (air out between wears). Hand wash cold with wool-specific detergent. Dry clean for structured pieces. | Lay flat to dry. Never hang wet wool (it stretches). Never use a dryer. | Store with cedar blocks to prevent moths. Roll instead of fold to reduce wrinkles [citation:4][citation:9] |
| **Silk** | Hand wash cold with mild, pH-neutral detergent. Never wring or twist. | Roll in a towel to remove excess water. Air dry away from sunlight. | Steam wrinkles (iron only on lowest setting with a pressing cloth) [citation:4][citation:9] |
| **Denim** | Wash inside out, cold water. Wash as infrequently as possible. | Air dry to preserve shape and color. | Spot clean small stains. Freezing can freshen between washes [citation:4] |
| **Polyester & Synthetics** | Machine wash warm. Avoid fabric softener (reduces moisture-wicking). | Tumble dry low or air dry. | Remove promptly to prevent static [citation:4] |

### Universal Fabric Care Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Read the care label** | It tells you everything: water temperature, drying method, whether it can be dry cleaned [citation:9] |
| **Sort by color + fabric weight** | Darks with darks. Lights with lights. Heavy denim shouldn't be washed with delicate silk [citation:4] |
| **Cold water is usually best** | Prevents shrinking, fading, and saves energy [citation:4] |
| **Don't overload the machine** | Clothes need room to move to get clean without excessive friction [citation:4] |
| **Air drying is gentler** | Dryers cause wear, shrinkage, and use energy. Hang or lay flat when possible [citation:9] |
