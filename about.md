# About

### Who am I?

Hey! I'm Cameron, a student and a developer

I created Nero's Guide to help students and teens find free resources online without having to dig through sketchy sites or paywalls.

## Why did I make this?

I got tired of seeing people pay for things that are available for free, or not knowing 
about tools that could make their life easier. This site is my way of sharing everything 
I've learned.
