## The Golden Rules {#golden-rules}

<div class="steps-grid">
  <div class="step-card">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Wait 24 hours</strong>
      <p>Impulse buying is the biggest budget killer. Sleep on it before purchasing.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Check price history</strong>
      <p>"Sales" are often fake markups followed by small discounts. Use CamelCamelCamel.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Use a separate shopping email</strong>
      <p>Keeps spam out of your main inbox and tracks who sold your data.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Clear your cookies</strong>
      <p>Some sites raise prices for repeat visitors. Browse incognito for big purchases.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## Scam Avoidance {#scams}

### Red Flags

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="warning-card">
    <strong>"Zelle / Venmo / CashApp only"</strong>
    <p>Hard pass. No buyer protection whatsoever.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="warning-card">
    <strong>Price is 50%+ lower than everywhere else</strong>
    <p>99% scam. If it looks too good to be true, it is.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="warning-card">
    <strong>Typos or "Kindly" in messages</strong>
    <p>Often overseas scam farms using scripts.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="warning-card">
    <strong>Seller asks for phone/email</strong>
    <p>They will send a fake payment phishing link.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Safe Payment Methods

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4><GuideIcon name="shield" :size="18" /> PayPal G&S</h4>
    <p>Buyer protection included. Avoid Friends &amp; Family for purchases.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4><GuideIcon name="shield" :size="18" /> Credit Card</h4>
    <p>Chargeback ability if item doesn't arrive or is misrepresented.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card">
    <h4><GuideIcon name="users" :size="18" /> Cash in person</h4>
    <p>Meet at a police station or bank for safety.</p>
  </div>
</div>
