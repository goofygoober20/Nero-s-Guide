## ⚡ The Golden Rules {#golden-rules}

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Wait 24 hours** | Impulse buying is the biggest budget killer. |
| **Check price history** | “Sales” are often fake markups followed by small discounts. |
| **Use a separate shopping email** | Keeps spam out of your main inbox and tracks who sold your data. |
| **Clear your cookies** | Some sites raise prices for repeat visitors. |

## 💰 Cashback & Rewards {#cashback}

Always check cashback rates *before* checking out. It’s free money.

- **Rakuten** – Pays via PayPal or check. Stack with in-store coupons.
- **TopCashback** – Often has higher rates than Rakuten.
- **Capital One Shopping** – Automatically tests coupon codes at checkout.
- **PayPal Honey** – Good for price drop alerts.
- **Swagbucks** – Cash back plus surveys if you have time to kill.

> **Stacking strategy:** Go to site via cashback portal → Apply coupon code → Pay with rewards credit card.

## 📦 Price Tracking & Drops {#price-tracking}

**CamelCamelCamel** – Best for Amazon. See if that “deal” is actually a deal.

**Keepa** – More detailed Amazon graphs than Camel.

**Slickdeals** – Community-driven. Set alerts for keywords.

**Honey Drops** – Works passively if you have Honey installed.

**Google Shopping** – Click “Compare prices.”

> **My rule:** If it wasn't on sale one month ago, I'm not buying it today.

## 🛒 Marketplace Tips (Amazon, eBay, etc.) {#marketplace}

### Amazon

- Check **“Other Sellers”** on the product page.
- Sort by **“New & Used”** for open-box.
- **CamelCamelCamel** before every purchase.

### eBay

- **Check photos**, not just description.
- Message seller for serial numbers.
- **Sort by distance** for local pickup.

### Temu / AliExpress / Shein

- Assume shipping takes 2-4 weeks.
- If price looks too good to be true, check reviews for photo proof.
- For small tools, electronics parts, or basic clothing – fine. For safety gear, hiking bags, or expensive anything – avoid.

## 🔒 Scam Avoidance {#scams}

### Red Flags

| Red Flag | What to do |
|----------|-------------|
| “Zelle / Venmo / CashApp only” | **Hard pass.** No buyer protection. |
| Price is 50%+ lower than everywhere else | 99% scam. |
| Typos in listing or “Kindly” in messages | Often overseas scam farms. |
| Seller asks for phone number or email | They will send a fake payment phishing link. |

### Safe Payment Methods

- **PayPal Goods & Services** – buyer protection (avoid F&F).
- **Credit Card** – chargeback ability.
- **Cash in person** – meet at a police station or bank.

## 💡 Summary Cheat Sheet

**Before buying anything:**

1. Do I need this, or do I just want the dopamine?
2. Check price history (CamelCamelCamel).
3. Search for coupon + cashback.
4. Check eBay for open-box or used.
5. Wait 24 hours. If still want it, buy with credit card.
