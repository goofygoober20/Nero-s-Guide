# How to Use This Site

::: info
**Whether you're here for the first time or the hundredth, this page covers everything you need to know about navigating Nero's Index, using the built-in tools, and finding what you need fast.**
:::
## Getting Started

Nero's Index is a collection of free, student-focused guides and browser-based tools. No signups, no paywalls, no data collected. Everything runs locally in your browser.

### What you'll find here

- **Guides**:  Curated resources on topics like finance, fitness, privacy, productivity, gaming, cooking, and more. Each guide is written to be concise and practical.
- **Tools**: Interactive calculators, timers, generators, and trackers that work entirely in your browser. No data leaves your device.

### How to navigate

The **homepage** shows all available guides in a grid. Use the **category pills** at the top to filter by topic. Click any guide card to open it. Each guide has a **table of contents** on the right side (desktop) or accessible through the hamburger menu (mobile).

### Tool tips

- Tools save your preferences locally (if you toggle settings like dark mode or confirm-before-reset)
- The **Password Generator** has milestone messages at different character lengths, try going to 64 characters
- The **Decision Wheel** has a hidden easter egg if you land on the number 42
- The **Box Breathing** tool tracks your completed cycles, reach 10 for a "Zen mode" notification

## Keyboard Shortcuts

You can navigate the site faster with keyboard shortcuts, **check them out in [settings](/settings.md)**

## Customizing Your Experience

Visit the **[Settings](/settings)** page to customize how the site looks and behaves:

- **Theme**: Switch between light, dark, or system preference
- **Accent color**: Choose from 20 accent colors to personalize the site
- **Font size**: Adjust text size for readability
- **Background brightness**: Control the overall brightness of the page
- **Reduced motion**: Disable animations for accessibility
- **Auto-hide nav**: Hide the navigation bar when scrolling down
- **Breadcrumbs**: Toggle the breadcrumb trail on guide pages
- **Confirm before reset**: Require confirmation before resetting tool data

## Privacy & Security

Your privacy is a priority. Here's what you should know:

- **No tracking**: This site uses no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking scripts
- **No data collection**: Tools hosted on this site run entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server
- **External links**: Some tools link to external sites (e.g., Pomodoro Timer). These are clearly marked with "External link" and may have their own data policies. Check their privacy policies before use
- **Feedback is anonymous**: The feedback form sends your message through a Discord webhook with no IP or metadata stored. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. Everything on this site is available without signing up.

**Is my data stored anywhere?**
No. Tools hosted on this site store preferences locally in your browser (via localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server. External tools (linked from this site) may have different policies, check their sites for details.

**Can I use this on my phone?**
Yes. The site is fully responsive and works on all screen sizes.

**How often is content updated?**
Guides are updated as needed. New guides and tools are added ocsasionally. Check the [Monthly Updates](/updates) page for the latest changes.

**I found a bug. How do I report it?**
Use the feedback bar at the bottom of any page, or visit the [Feedback](/feedback) page.
