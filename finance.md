## Budgeting Apps

<div class="card-grid-3">
  <a href="https://www.ynab.com/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>YNAB (You Need A Budget)</h4>
    <p>The gold standard. Every dollar gets a job. Syncs with your bank. $14.99/month (free for 34 days). Life-changing for many people.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://actualbudget.org/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Actual Budget</h4>
    <p>Open source, self-hosted alternative to YNAB. One-time payment of ~$4/month or free if you self-host. Full control of your data.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://mint.intuit.com/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Mint</h4>
    <p>Free budgeting app by Intuit. Automatically categorizes transactions, tracks bills, and shows net worth. Good for passive tracking.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://lunchmoney.app/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Lunch Money</h4>
    <p>Modern budgeting app with beautiful UI. Supports multiple currencies, recurring transactions, and rules. $10/month.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://copilot.money/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Copilot</h4>
    <p>Mac/iOS only, but beautiful and intuitive. Syncs with Plaid, supports investments, and has great visual reports. $13/month.</p>
  </a>
  <a href="https://plaintextaccounting.org/" target="_blank" class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Plain text accounting</h4>
    <p>Ledger / hledger / Beancount — track every transaction in a text file. Powerful but a learning curve. Free and fully private.</p>
  </a>
</div>

## Saving Strategies

<div class="steps-grid">
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Build an Emergency Fund</strong>
      <p>Save 3-6 months of expenses in a high-yield savings account. This is your safety net for job loss or unexpected expenses.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Automate Your Savings</strong>
      <p>Set up automatic transfers on payday. You can't spend what you don't see. Start with 10-20% of your income.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Use the 50/30/20 Rule</strong>
      <p>50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings/debt. Adjust based on your situation, but it's a solid starting framework.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-card reveal">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <div>
      <strong>High-Yield Savings Account</strong>
      <p>Put your savings in a HYSA (3-5% APY) instead of a regular bank account (0.01%). Ally, SoFi, Marcus are good options.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## Investing Basics

<div class="card-grid-2">
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Index Funds / ETFs</h4>
    <p>The simplest and most effective investment. Buy the whole market (VTI, VOO, VT) instead of individual stocks. Low fees, diversified, historically ~7-10% annual returns.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Retirement Accounts</h4>
    <p>Max out tax-advantaged accounts first: 401(k) (especially if employer matches), IRA (Roth or Traditional), HSA (triple tax-advantaged if eligible).</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Compound Interest</h4>
    <p>Your money grows exponentially over time. $500/month invested at 8% for 30 years = ~$680,000. Start early and stay consistent.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shared-card reveal">
    <h4>Dollar Cost Averaging</h4>
    <p>Invest a fixed amount regularly regardless of market conditions. Avoids the stress of timing the market. Set and forget.</p>
  </div>
</div>
